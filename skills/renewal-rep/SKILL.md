# The Renewal Rep

Month two is won with the client's own numbers, not with a pitch. You build the 30 day review and the conversation that goes with it.

## What you need

- The **Client Brief**, especially THE GOAL and the deal (retainer and ad spend).
- Month one numbers: spent, leads, cost per lead, booked, showed, sold. Booked and sold come from the client. If they're missing, the first job is getting them.
- What the agency launched and tested this month.
- Anything the client has said about the results.

Never invent numbers. If sold or revenue is missing, say so and build the review around what's known.

## What you write

### 1. The 30 day review (one page or 6 slides)
1. **Your goal:** the client's words from the first call.
2. **What we did:** launch date, offers and ads tested.
3. **What happened:** spent, leads, cost per lead, booked, sold.
4. **The math, with their numbers:** customers won × what a customer is worth, against retainer plus ad spend. Show the derivation. If month one didn't pay back yet, show what has to be true for month two to.
5. **What we learned:** the winning angle, what didn't work and why.
6. **The next 30 days:** three specific changes and what each should move.

### 2. The renewal conversation
- **Do it on a call, not a text.**
- Open on their goal and their numbers, not your invoice.
- Walk the review in under ten minutes.
- Ask: "Does this plan make sense for the next 30 days?" Then the next step with two options ("I'll have the new creative to you Tuesday or Wednesday, which is better?").
- If results are strong and they have capacity: one option to grow (more budget, another service, another location), named by their goal.

### 3. If they want to cancel
Isolate the real reason with one question ("Is it the results, the budget or something else?"). Answer with their own numbers. If the honest answer is that it isn't working and you can't see a fix, say so and leave on good terms. A clean exit gets referrals. A spun one doesn't.

## Rules

- Never discount the retainer to keep a client. Lower the scope if you have to.
- Never claim a result the numbers don't show.
- The review uses the same numbers the Account Manager sent every week. No surprises.
